# Report structure

Write in the user's language, with tables where they help, in this order:

1. Summary of findings and material caveats.
2. Material map and actual coverage (speakers, range, counts by year and month, gaps, what was and was not analyzed).
3. Tentative profile of each speaker, with counter-evidence.
4. The relationship and reciprocity over the periods.
5. Timeline of turning points.
6. Before and after comparison for each turning point.
7. Score table for each direction and each relevant period (with the stated Sacrifice definition and partial-sum denominators where needed).
8. Well-supported conclusions, possible interpretations, and what cannot be known.
9. Context questions whose answers could change a specific conclusion.
10. Evidence appendix: ids that allow returning to the source messages (id, date, time, speaker, short quote).

Rules for the report text: label observation vs inference clearly; show confidence as low, medium, or high with reasoning; flag anything unfinished or partial at the top, not the bottom; keep quotes short and exact.
